Warranty. This is a sample policy for a fictional store, Trailhead Outfitters.

Every item we make under the Trailhead label carries a two-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, counted from the day it was delivered to you. A defect is a fault in how the item was made: a seam that comes apart under normal use, a zipper that separates when it is closed, a buckle that cracks without being dropped, a waterproof coating that peels from the fabric. Items from other brands that we sell carry the maker's own warranty, and we help you file a claim with them.

The warranty does not cover normal wear: fabric that thins or pills over years of use, color that fades in the sun, soles that wear down from walking. It does not cover damage from accidents, from fire or chemicals, or from repairs or changes made by anyone other than us. It does not cover an item used for a purpose it was not made for, such as a day pack loaded past its stated weight limit.

To make a warranty claim, sign in, open the order and choose Warranty claim, or email warranty@trailhead.example with the order number and photos of the fault. We answer within three business days. If we agree the item is defective, we repair it, replace it with the same item, or, when neither is possible, give a refund of the price you paid. A repair takes about three weeks, and we pay the shipping both ways.

If you bought the item as a gift, the person who received it can make the claim with the gift receipt. Without any receipt, we look the order up by the name and address it shipped to. Items bought from a reseller are covered only when the reseller is listed on our website as an authorized dealer.

Repairs outside the warranty. We also repair Trailhead items that the warranty does not cover, for a fee: a zipper replacement costs $25, a seam repair $15, and a patch on a tear up to four inches long $20. Send the item with the repair form from our website, and we email a quote before any work starts. If you decline the quote, we send the item back at no charge.
