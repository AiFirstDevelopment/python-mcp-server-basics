Price match. This is a sample policy for a fictional store, Trailhead Outfitters.

If you find an item we sell at a lower price from another retailer in the United States, we match that price. The item must be identical: the same brand, model, size and color, new and in stock at the other retailer. Ask for a price match before you buy by calling us or chatting with us, or show the lower price at the register in any of our stores. We check the other retailer's current price on their website or by phone.

We match prices from retailers that sell from their own stores or websites. We do not match marketplace sellers on sites that list items from many independent sellers, auction sites, outlet or clearance prices, prices that need a membership or a coupon code, bundle deals, or prices with an error the retailer has said it will not honor. We do not match our own past prices.

After you buy. If our own price drops within 14 days after you buy, we refund the difference. Contact us with the order number, and we refund it to your original payment method. This applies to our own price only: if another retailer lowers its price after you buy, we do not refund the difference. Price adjustments are limited to one per item.

Price match and Rewards. A matched price earns Rewards points on the matched amount, not the original price. Points can still be used on a price-matched order.
